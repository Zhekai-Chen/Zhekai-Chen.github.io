## About Me

I am an undergraduate student in the Computer Science and Technology program at Zhejiang University, expected to graduate in 2025. In 2023, I joined the National Key Lab of CAD&CG, where I conducted research on generative models under the mentorship of Professors [Chunhua Shen](https://cshen.github.io/) and [Hao Chen](https://stan-haochen.github.io/). I had the honor of working as a research intern in Professor [Tong Zhang](https://tongzhang-ml.org/)'s group at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) during the summer of 2024, gaining valuable insights into the cutting-edge developments in the field of multimodality. Currently, I am working as a research intern in Alibaba Tongyi Laboratory, under the supervision of Dr. [Shiwei Zhang](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=ZO3OQ-8AAAAJ&hl=zh-CN).

I aim to empower individuals to express creativity effortlessly, eliminating the need for specialized skills or software. My focus lies in three key areas:

1. Controllable Image & Video Generation: Enabling intuitive adjustments for users to shape their visual content seamlessly.
2. Advancing LLM/MLLM Capabilities: Bridging human intent and machine interpretation, transforming thoughts into actionable outputs.
3. Enhancing Human-Computer Interaction: Making communication with AI more natural and intuitive, utilizing physical media without complex operations.

**I am seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2025. If you are interested, please contact me.**
